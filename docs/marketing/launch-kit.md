## 5. 7-day launch plan (accounting for the 16+ trial)

**What we know (GDD R-1, legal 3.6; verify on the live Roblox page):** to reach players under 16, the game must pass the Kids/Select evaluation. That needs the creator age check + 2SV, plus either the one-time refundable per-game fee (about 1,000 R$, unverified) or 2 months of Plus/Premium. Then a **trial visible only to age-checked users 16+** runs until **250 unique plays by highly engaged, age-checked users within 60 days**, plus a safety review. So at launch our real target (9-14) cannot see the game, and early numbers come from 16+ players only.

**What we do not know (owner must confirm; I cannot see the account):** whether the age check and 2SV are done, whether the fee or membership route was chosen (gate 3, Robux), what Roblox counts as "highly engaged", and where the Creator Hub shows the progress counter. The plan below does not depend on the answers but the owner should find them on Day 0.

**Principles:**
- 250 engaged plays in 60 days is about 4 per day on average. It is a modest bar. The risk is not volume, it is that the right (16+, age-checked) people find the game. There is no point rushing paid traffic on day 1.
- Only real, honest play counts. No alt accounts, no paying friends, no "play for a reward" campaigns (Integrity, and ads rules about incentives). Ask real 16+ people to genuinely play.
- No ads this week (see ads-plan.md): NL-1 open, trial status unclear, small sample anyway.
- Do not judge fun or monetization from trial data alone (GDD R-1). We read it as a smoke test: does the first 5 minutes work, does it save, do purchases process.
- The weekly Saturday rhythm still goes ahead (Update 1 on Saturday), but it is allowed to slip if the build is not stable.

## 6. Metrics to watch (in the order of the funnel)

The game already logs these (GDD 10, keyed by UserId only): time to first win, first upgrade, first pod, first rank-up, first rebirth, session length, every purchase. Ranges are **rough benchmarks for the genre and not facts about our game**. Early data is from 16+ players on a trial and may be as small as a few dozen users, so one or two players can swing a percentage a lot. Always read counts together with the percentages.

| Metric | What it tells us | Target for us | Rough "healthy" | If it is low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Time to first win** | Whether the opening works | **Under 60 s** (GDD sim: 0:36 at 5 taps/s, 0:49 at 3). Median under 60 s, 90th percentile under 2 min | Same | Tutorial arrows or the MOG button are unclear; check mobile tap area and camera. Fix before anything else. |
| **D1 retention** | Do they come back tomorrow | 25%+ | Roblox clickers/simulators often land roughly 15-30% D1, 20%+ is OK, 30%+ is strong (rough) | Daily reward not visible, no reason to return, a save problem. Make sure the Day 2 teaser and the reward calendar show after the first session. |
| **Session length** | Depth of the first visit | 15-25 min average (GDD design) | Roughly 12-25 min is typical for the genre (rough) | Under 8 min: the first rank-up (about 3 min) or first egg (about 4 min) is not landing, or lag. Over 40 min with low D1: pacing may be too grindy. |
| **First purchase (payer conversion)** | Whether the shop is found and trusted | 1-3% of players buy something in the first week | Rough genre range 1-5%; younger or trial audiences often lower | Under 0.5%: shop buttons not seen after the first win, Starter Pack pop-up (9 R$) not shown, price not clear. Check the Starter Pack pop-up after the first rank-up. |
| **D7 retention** | Long-term hook | 8%+ | Roughly 5-12% (rough) | Needs the Day 7 cohort. Low D7 means rebirth, pods and duels are not compelling; the Saturday update is the lever. |
| **Rank-up and rebirth funnel** | Where players quit | Rank 2 by about 3-4 min; first rebirth by about 10-15 min | GDD 4.7 | Big drop between two steps shows the step to fix. |

**Also record, each time we look:** unique players, concurrent peak (CCU), trial engaged-play counter, likes/dislikes ratio, errors in the output log (qa). ARPPU, payer conversion and CCU are tracked in more detail in `metrics.md` (to be written after launch day, not part of T-085).

**Rule for reporting:** I say "too early to tell" when there are fewer than about 100 users in a cohort.
