## Per-game notes

### +1 Mog Evolution (reference)
- Source: owner brief [BRIEF], RW snippet, [AT] event page.
- Core loop / twist: see brief section 1. Click Appeal, hammer upgrades, NPC mogs for Wins, Rebirth/Ascend, 4 worlds, idle "treadmill" stations x1 to x999, PvP with paid Revenge, Ranked.
- Cadence: weekly numbered updates, Saturday "Admin Abuse" live event with sign-ups. An event page said it had run 3 times by Oct 21 (unverified). This is the standard we must match.
- Thumbnail/icon: split before/after face, ugly on red vs chad on green. Trailer opens on idle stations.
- Does well: 50 impulse products with a 1 R$ entry; constant aspiration visible from spawn (station row, VIP station, limited-character statue); always-on leaderboard; weekly event rhythm; 98% like rating.
- Does badly: first mog is unclear (players wander for minutes); purchase prompts open from tutorial-path pads; HUD is a wall of purchase buttons; ~100 clicks before the first fight with no excitement in between; CCU falling since the Sep 20 peak (unverified).
- Search note: many sites confuse it with "+1 Magic Evolution" (a different game). That is evidence that "+1 X Evolution" titles are already confusable with each other. We must avoid the whole "+1 ... Evolution" shape.

### +1 Mog Per Click
- Source: [RT]. Created by "LOL Strong Game", 2026-02-18, ~3.4-3.6M visits, 265-271K favorites, 95.1% rating (conflicting snapshots). Minimal maturity.

- Does well: first in the niche (Feb 2026), strong like ratio. Does badly: no visible differentiation, no found update rhythm (unverified).

### Mog or Die
- Source: [CODES] (many guides, Aug-Sep 2026), Rolimons snippet.
- Loop: the player starts as a tiny "SUB5" and grows to top "MOGGER". Four stats: Height, Face, Frame, Bodyfat (drinking milk raises height). Mog Battles against NPCs and players; defeated NPCs can be displayed at the player's base. Currencies: MogPoints, Lucky Spins (random paid/free rolls).
- Twist: base with trophy NPCs, spin mechanic, and visible stat growth in height.
- Cadence: "UPD 5" in title; codes refreshed Aug, Sep 2026. Codes tied to like milestones (300 likes, 1.5K likes).
- Does well: more than one stat gives build choice; showing defeated NPCs is a nice bragging hook.
- Does badly: Lucky Spins imply paid or free randomness, which is a legal flag for us (odds disclosure). "Bodyfat" and "Frame" are body-image mechanics that sit near the content rules in the brief section 5.
- Earlier search snippet said a Mog Battle there lets a winner erase a rival's progress. I could not confirm this. Unverified.

## Cross-genre patterns (all unverified except where marked [BRIEF])
- Title formula: "+1 <thing> <noun>" is saturated: "+1 Mog Evolution", "+1 Mog Per Click", "+1 Mog Battle", "+1 Looks Per Click", "+1 Magic Evolution", "+1 Money Per Click", "+1 Strength Per Click". Pages mix them up.
- Codes tied to likes/visits are universal (retention checklist in brief section 4 already covers this).
- Third-party code and stat sites are a free discovery channel; names that are unique rank easier.
- Nobody found in my results shows a visible before/after avatar change as the game's actual feature (it is only in the reference's thumbnail art) [partially BRIEF].

## Gaps nobody fills
1. Tutorial clarity. The reference leaves players wandering without a first win [BRIEF]. No competitor I found advertises a "first win in under a minute" experience. This is our cheapest edge and costs no extra art.
2. A real visible avatar transformation. Competitors show a stat number or a label rank; the before/after exists only in thumbnails. Nobody (in my results) transforms the player's actual avatar step by step in-game.
3. A fair, non-pay-to-win ranked layer. The reference sells a paid Revenge and big multipliers into a Ranked mode [BRIEF]. A bracketed ranked mode (rank-matched, cosmetic-only purchases) is unclaimed.
4. Cooperative / team play. All competitors found are solo-grind with PvP bolt-ons; no team mog mode or crew goals surfaced. 12-player servers leave room for shared objectives.
5. Retention past week 4. The reference lost ~55% of CCU in 30 days and +1 Looks Per Click dropped about 93% from peak (both unverified). A story, season or collection layer that gives a reason to return is open.
6. Cartoon-safe, parent-friendly tone. Looksmaxxing is under public criticism for kids (articles on body image and unsafe "tips" turned up in search). Competitors use "Bodyfat", "Frame", "Subhuman" style labels. A clearly silly, positive-spin game (confidence, style, "aura", glow-up) is unfilled and helps with the Minimal rating and the lawyer.
7. Funny physics payoff. Nobody in my results markets ragdoll or comedic knockback as the headline. This also matches the brief's physics-programmer scope.
8. Discoverable home: most competitors have only a Discord, no wiki. A ready wiki-style page, update log and codes page at launch is cheap and gives us SEO-style discoverability that others miss (inference).

## Positioning notes

### Title rules
- Do not use "+1", "Mog Evolution", "Evolution", "Per Click", "Mog Battle", "Mog or Die", "Looksmax", "Frame Mog" or any influencer name. "Mog" alone is a genre keyword and fine in description/tags, but prefer a title that works without it so we are not buried in the same search results.
- Prefer a short, unique, pronounceable name that is 1-3 words, has no number prefix, and has a clear visual (glow-up, transformation, aura, level-up).
- Title directions to explore in `launch-kit.md` (5 options will follow once the owner picks the twist; no trademark check done yet):
  - Glow-up or makeover words ("Glow Up", "Makeover", "Rizz")
  - Aura / charisma words
  - Rank-ladder or "tier" words
  - A mascot-led name (silly character)
- Every option must be checked for clashes and sent to the lawyer before it is shown as final.

### Thumbnail angles distinct from the reference
- The reference uses a split face, ugly-on-red vs chad-on-green. Avoid red/green split, avoid face-only close-ups, avoid the same two-panel layout.
- Angles that read at thumbnail size (small, mobile): (a) a 4-5 step "staircase" of one avatar growing from a goofy outfit to a glowing hero; (b) a mid-air ragdoll knockback with a "comic POW" burst; (c) a crew of 3-4 avatars in matching outfits (only if the team twist wins).
- Use our own art (Roblox-style avatars), saturated blue/orange or purple/yellow, not red/green. Minimal or no text; a single large number or icon is better than words.
- Icon: a single character head or silhouette with an aura glow on a plain colored background; no text.

### Safety and legal watch-list for later steps
- Real-person names or likenesses: flagged (Clavicular title). Avoid in title, assets, codes, NPC names, social posts.
- Lucky Spins and random rolls (Mog or Die): any paid random item needs odds disclosure; the brief already says no paid random items. Keep it that way.
- Body-image terms ("Subhuman", "Bodyfat", "Frame") are risky for a kid audience and the Minimal label. Use cartoon framing (style, aura, gym gear, skincare per brief section 5).
- Purchase prompts from walking over pads: drop it (brief section 4 already does).
